# HD model

Official DJI 3D model.

The source model is in `source/` and `textures/`. The model files are not part of the repository. `npm run prepare:hd` creates the local runtime GLB in `public/3d_model/HD/`, preserving materials and textures, converting source units to metres (0.0005 m per source unit) and recentering the scene. The Air 2S source has about 604 source units across its motor diagonals; 0.0005 m per unit matches the declared 0.302 m span.

`rotors.json` declares, in source coordinates, each rotor's blade groups, motor centre and spin axis, and the motor parts that turn with it. The motors tilt a few degrees from vertical, and preparation turns each rotor pivot onto its motor's axis. Each axis was measured as the normal of a plane fitted to the lower rim of the motor bell, and each centre as the centre of the bell wall about that axis. Of the listed motor parts, triangles that look the same at every angle about the axis, such as rims, walls and shafts, stay on the fixed motor and draw solid. The rest, such as the prop mount, clips and screws, move under the rotor pivot: they turn with the blades and, above the display alias limit, give way to the semi-transparent propeller sweep. A motor part may name components of its mesh, numbered as for the gimbal. `gimbal.json` declares separate yaw-arm, roll-yoke and camera-housing pitch joints. The source combines these shells into one mesh, and merges the lens elements and front glass into two body meshes, so preparation partitions disconnected components by triangle indices while retaining all vertex attributes, materials and textures. Component numbers are ordered by descending welded vertex count; expected triangle counts make a changed source fail explicitly. Unassigned components remain attached to their original fixed mount. Only the camera housing, lens and front glass follow pitch; the roll yoke follows roll, and the suspension arm follows yaw. Each pivot has its own source-coordinate centre, and preparation preserves the complete rest-world geometry.
